## Unmeasured or not established

No verified city-image mAP/IDF1, lane mapping accuracy, visual queue MAE, independent calibrated city twin, current detector-to-camera fusion, field traffic savings, bus/pedestrian delay improvements, controller speedup, city-wide feed uptime or human usability results.
