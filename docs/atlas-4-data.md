# Five-city historical count ingestion

This increment acquired **52,679 actual published historical motor-vehicle count bins** across all five required cities. These are source measurements, with the limitations below, and remain separate from October 2026 camera snapshots. No historical count is presented as a current observation. No simulator calibration accuracy is established by acquiring counts.

The canonical archives are [data-index.json](../artifacts/cities/v4/data-index.json) and `data-{city}.json.gz` in the same directory. [data-coverage.json](../artifacts/cities/v4/data-coverage.json) describes each site, dates, actual zero counts, missing-bin gaps, previews and geographic distance to the existing modeled roads. ## Official sources and data quality

**Toronto:** [City turning movement data](https://open.toronto.ca/dataset/traffic-volumes-at-intersections-for-all-modes/), CKAN resource `262469c2-abfe-4756-9068-4ea5c7ba1af7`. Four Bay Street intersections have separately dated historical studies. Each normalized count sums cars, trucks and buses across the four approaches and three turns; pedestrian and bicycle measurements are excluded. The additional [movement archive](../artifacts/cities/v4/data-toronto-movements.json.gz) preserves all 832 source rows, study IDs, explicit approach/turn/mode counts, locations, observation intervals and response digests. No camera-date fusion occurs. Toronto source-local civil timestamps remain naive, with the `America/Toronto` basis recorded, rather than inventing source UTC offsets. Redistribution uses the [Open Government Licence ? Toronto](https://open.toronto.ca/open-data-licence/) with attribution.

**London:** [DfT documented raw-count API](https://roadtraffic.dft.gov.uk/api-documentation), count points 26430, 18600, 26775 and 28214 near Holborn. These are actual manual hourly counts, distinct from annual-average estimates. Both observed directions are summed per site-hour exactly once; motor vehicles include motorcycles and goods vehicles and exclude pedal cycles. Sparse survey days are not converted into a continuous detector series. Survey-hour timestamps retain the explicit `Europe/London` local basis without invented offsets. DfT publishes its data under [UK Open Government Licence v3.0](https://roadtraffic.dft.gov.uk/count-points/26430).

**Seattle:** [15-minute study bins](https://data.seattle.gov/Transportation/Traffic-Counts-Studies-by-15-Minute-Bins/gi49-5uh6) joined to the [official study registry](https://data.seattle.gov/Transportation/Traffic-Counts-by-Study/xucb-vzhc). Source dates come from actual count-year/month/day/hour/minute fields, rather than database modification times. TOTAL FLOW aggregate studies are excluded to prevent counting directional studies twice. Seven negative `-1` source count sentinels are omitted and preserved explicitly in `rejected_records`; they are never coerced to zero. Modality varies among pneumatic tubes and video counters. Physical locations are source road descriptions, with no invented coordinates. The published dataset metadata designates Public Domain; SDOT attribution is retained.

**Austin:** [Camera Traffic Counts](https://data.austintexas.gov/Transportation-and-Mobility/Camera-Traffic-Counts/sh59-i6y9). Published GRIDSMART light/heavy and movement bins are summed per intersection and interval. Field metadata explicitly labels `read_date` UTC, so normalized timestamps retain `+00:00`. This dataset is **discontinued**: the City says these devices are no longer maintained, measurements use automated vision and have not been validated, and some speed measurements were uncalibrated. No speed accuracy or independently annotated count accuracy is claimed. Published [detector registry](https://data.austintexas.gov/Transportation-and-Mobility/Traffic-Detectors/qpuw-8eeb) coordinates were associated only when the candidate coordinates agree; an initially ambiguous 5th Street substring lookup was rejected, then resolved using exact normalized intersection names. All four registry coordinates are now supported. The registry is contemporary and historical coordinate stability is not independently verified. The [City open-data terms](https://data.austintexas.gov/stories/s/ranj-cccq) offer City-created data without restriction unless metadata says otherwise; the count table supplies no separate restrictive licence. City and department attribution are retained. This is aggregate municipal detector data, with no identification or re-identification.

**Calgary:** [Permanent-station traffic counts](https://data.calgary.ca/Transportation-Transit/Traffic-Counts-at-Permanent-stations/vuyp-sbjp) matched by published segment IDs to the [station registry](https://data.calgary.ca/Transportation-Transit/Permanent-station-locations-for-Traffic-counts/sqwx-tjsy). Quarter-hour volumes are preserved; opposite directions remain distinct channels. Four nearby operational locations are selected using a declared geographic rule, including the one-way 5 Avenue flyover with only one published segment. Source-naive time has no encoded timezone: `America/Edmonton` is a documented interpretation requiring confirmation before weather fusion. Counter measurement accuracy is not independently field-audited. [Open Calgary terms](https://data.calgary.ca/stories/s/Open-Calgary-Terms-of-Use/u45n-7awa) explicitly allow reuse and distribution under the Open Government Licence ? City of Calgary, subject to attribution; attribution is included.

## Engineering safeguards

`HistoricalClient` reuses the bounded official-source transport: approved HTTPS hosts, redirect validation, bounded response bytes, finite retries and per-origin rate limiting. It adds a digest-checked 24-hour disk cache. Cache hits retain the **original retrieval time**; a failed expired-cache refresh raises an error instead of displaying old data as newly retrieved. Every request records outcome and check/retrieval time. Successful responses retain byte counts and response digests; earlier parser/negative-count failures remain in index attempt history. A one-time successful check does not establish long-term uptime.

Every normalized observation retains source, city, site/location, known observation time and time basis, retrieval time, native interval, count units, licence, historical freshness and explicit unquantified source-measurement uncertainty. Missing periods remain absent; the consumer must require contiguous bins. The normalizer rejects negative, fractional, nonfinite and boolean counts, duplicate site/time/direction bins, missing locations and unsupported intervals. No artificial zero-fill, temporal interpolation, pseudo-current forecast or camera FOV mapping is applied.

## Calibration boundary

Three Toronto locations fall within approximately 3?7 metres of existing modeled roads; Dundas is approximately 399 metres away. This is geographic proximity, not verified approach-to-edge correspondence or a calibrated twin. London sites are 1.2?1.5 km outside the modeled corridor. Calgary stations are 0.7?2.2 km away. Austin supported registry locations are 0.2?1.1 km away. Seattle lacks count-table geographic coordinates. Historic geometry and municipal signal timing are unverified. The [actual SUMO junction diagnostic](../artifacts/cities/v4/data-network-alignment.json) verifies all five imported networks against known count coordinates and exposes unsupported topology. For example, Bay/Queen has four source approach labels but its nearest modeled node has three incoming and three outgoing short clustered segments; source movements require careful clustered-node mapping. All five city networks therefore remain **exploratory**, pending compatible movement mapping and independent field measurements for flow, speed, travel time and queues.

These inputs support new chronological count-forecast experiments. They do not inherit METR-LA highway accuracy, five-city camera accuracy, independent queue error or field control savings. Train/validation/test separation belongs to the separately frozen forecasting or simulator protocol; ingestion itself fits no model and chooses no policy.

## Native city-count shadow inference

`backend/atlas/city_runtime_v4.py` executes the validation-selected local learned model for each city, using its actual trained checkpoint and native count units. It requires three finite, nonnegative, whole-count measurements from the same known site/direction at exactly the source cadence. UTC and source-local timestamps retain their verified semantics. A still-image visible-object count is rejected as a different measurement quantity.

The versioned forecasting evidence pins the runtime-manifest digest, which pins checkpoint, source-data, protocol and causal-feature hashes. Each call verifies those bytes and the frozen validation selection before consulting bounded caches. Neural models load with `torch.load(weights_only=True)`; HGB loads only the locally trained server-controlled digest-pinned joblib file. Requests cannot supply model paths, uploaded pickle payloads or alternate checkpoints. Source caches retain small site/channel registries after parsing, rather than holding all 52,679 observations in memory. HGB OpenMP execution is limited to four threads under a runtime lock.

Graph messages accept only known source neighbors with exactly matching historical timestamps, interval, direction and time basis, and known coordinates within 1,500 metres. Missing neighbors use the trained missing-message mask; distant or temporally mismatched neighbors are rejected. This remains an undirected geographic graph, not a surveyed road-flow topology. Caller-supplied measurements are explicitly labeled as not independently verified by the inference endpoint.

Returned forecasts retain the next native interval, model/data/protocol/feature and actual loaded-runtime versions, frozen validation-residual interval, observed chronological holdout coverage, a persistence shadow comparison and a training-history z-score monitor. The heuristic `|z| > 3` flag has no independently measured OOD detection accuracy. Toronto and London regressions versus persistence remain visible; the native endpoint does not automatically promote a candidate, retrain it, map it to snapshot demand or actuate a field signal.

Eight additional isolated runtime behavior tests cover actual neural forward/scale decoding, trusted HGB loading, uncertainty and regression labels, source/checkpoint/manifest tampering even after caching, unsafe checkpoint paths, temporal and spatial neighbor validation, snapshot-stock rejection, cadence/timezone semantics, invalid counts and extreme-input monitoring. `scripts/verify_city_runtime_v4.py` separately verifies actual authenticated native HTTP inference for all five cities, exact frozen first-test preview parity, unauthorized access rejection and snapshot-stock rejection; repeated warm request profiles are published in `city-runtime-profile.json`. Repeated identical histories and one serial client are not a concurrent-load SLA or a new forecast accuracy benchmark.
